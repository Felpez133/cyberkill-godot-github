Sewer Rat & Pest Pack
=====================

16 transparent PNG sprites (32x32): giant rat, rat king, cockroach swarm, sewer gator with animation frames

How to use:
- Import the PNGs into your engine (Godot, Unity, GameMaker).
- Frames are named <creature>_<action>_<n>; loop at 6-10 fps.
- 32x32 with transparency; scale with nearest-neighbor filtering.
